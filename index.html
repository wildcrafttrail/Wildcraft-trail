Tips & Alternatives

Traditional vs. Modern

Fire: A ferro rod is excellent for practice; carry a reliable lighter as a backup.

Shelter: A lightweight tarp is often more practical and lower-impact than building from natural materials.

Rope: Learn traditional cordage, but use purpose-rated modern cord for tasks requiring known strength.

Tools: Start with a dependable knife and saw rather than buying every gadget.
