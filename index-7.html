The Frontier Dictionary

Words from the Wilderness

Search essential bushcraft terms. This small glossary will grow with the catalog.

Tinder — Fine, dry material that catches a spark or flame easily.
Ferro Rod — A ferrocerium fire starter that produces hot sparks when scraped.
Feather Stick — A piece of wood carved into thin attached curls to help ignite kindling.
Tarp — A waterproof sheet used for simple outdoor shelters.
Batoning — Splitting small wood by carefully striking a knife spine with another piece of wood; it can damage unsuitable knives.

Tips & Practical Alternatives

Traditional vs. Modern

Fire: Ferro rod for practice; a reliable lighter for convenience and emergencies.

Shelter: Natural materials where legally permitted; a lightweight tarp for low-impact camping.

Rope: Natural cordage for learning; certified modern cord for tasks that require known strength.

Instructor's Note

Good bushcraft is not about owning the most expensive equipment. It is about understanding what you carry, knowing your limits and making safe choices outdoors.

Always follow local access, camping and fire regulations.
